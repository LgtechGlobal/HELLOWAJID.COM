# Odisha Tourism & Similipal National Park Website

A premium, modern, responsive Tour & Travel website showcasing the natural wonders, tiger reserves, roaring waterfalls, pristine golden beaches, and ancient cultural heritage of Odisha, India, with primary spotlight on **Similipal National Park**.

## 🌲 Tech Stack
- **React.js** (Vite)
- **Tailwind CSS** (Custom Nature Palette: Forest Green, Olive Green, Cream, White & Earthy Brown)
- **React Router DOM v6** (6 dedicated, standalone routes)
- **Lucide React** (Modern iconography)
- **Framer Motion** (Smooth transitions, modals, interactive tabs, and layout animations)

---

## 🗺️ Routes & Pages
1. **Home (`/`)**: Cinematic full-screen hero with scroll-down indicator, Similipal primary destination showcase, 10 iconic destination photography cards, "Why Choose Us" features, and gallery preview.
2. **About Us (`/about`)**: Odisha tourism overview, 4 core pillars (Nature, Wildlife, Beaches, Heritage), deep-forest photographic Similipal section, and Mission & Vision.
3. **Destinations (`/destinations`)**: 18 curated destinations across 5 categories (Wildlife & Nature, Waterfalls, Beaches & Lakes, Hills & Adventure, Culture & Heritage) with interactive category filtering and detailed "Explore More" modals.
4. **Gallery (`/gallery`)**: Masonry-style high-definition photography gallery with categories (All, Wildlife, Waterfalls, Beaches, Mountains, Heritage) and a fullscreen lightbox with keyboard navigation and captions.
5. **Packages (`/packages`)**: Verified tour package cards (Similipal Adventure 2D/1N, Similipal Nature Explorer 3D/2N, Odisha Nature Tour 5D/4N, Odisha Complete Explorer 7D/6N) with modal itinerary breakdowns and direct booking action.
6. **Contact Us (`/contact`)**: Landscape banner, complete booking inquiry form (with automatic pre-selection from tour cards), travel desk coordinates, and Google Maps embed for Similipal Biosphere Reserve.
